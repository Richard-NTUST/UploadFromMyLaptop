# Meeting Notes

> **What should I record during a research meeting?**

This template helps researchers prepare for meetings, review progress, and define measurable action items.

Every meeting should **begin by reviewing pending tasks and end with new action items**.

Each action item should have a clear owner, measurable deliverable, due date, and supporting evidence.

---

## Why?

A research meeting should produce measurable action items.

Meeting notes should record only the essential information needed to continue the research.
